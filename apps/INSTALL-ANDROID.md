# Install Auduio on an Android phone or tablet

Auduio isn't in the Play Store yet, so you install the app file (an "APK") yourself. It takes about two minutes.

**You need:** Android 7 or newer and the file `Auduio-0.5.1-android.apk`.

## 1. Get the file onto your phone
Pick whichever is easiest:
- Email it to yourself, or put it in Google Drive, then open it on the phone. **Or**
- Plug the phone into a computer with a USB cable and copy the file into the phone's **Download** folder.

## 2. Open the file
1. On the phone, open the **Files** app (it may be called "My Files" or "Downloads").
2. Tap **Auduio-0.5.1-android.apk**.

## 3. Allow the install (first time only)
Android will say something like *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."*
1. Tap **Settings** in that message.
2. Turn on **Allow from this source**.
3. Press the **Back** button.
4. Tap **Install**.

If you see a *"Play Protect"* warning, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. The warning appears because the app isn't from the Play Store.

## 4. Start Auduio
1. Tap **Open**, or find the **Auduio** icon on your home screen or in the app list.
2. Tap **Tap to start audio**.
3. The first time you record, Android asks *"Allow Auduio to record audio?"* Tap **While using the app**.

That's it. The phone layout opens by itself. Use headphones when you want to hear yourself while recording.

## Good to know
- **Updating:** install the newer APK the same way. Your projects stay on the phone.
- **Microphone refused by mistake?** Go to Settings > Apps > Auduio > Permissions > Microphone > Allow.
- **The screen stays on** while Auduio is open, because Android pauses apps (and a running recording) when the screen turns off. Switch to another app, or press the power button, when you're done.
- **Your data:** projects are saved inside the app on this phone. Use **More > Save / share** to keep a copy elsewhere.
- **Removing the app:** press and hold the Auduio icon, then tap Uninstall.
